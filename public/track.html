FAQ
How long does a clean take?

Standard is 3–5 days, Express is 2–3 days, and Next Day is back with you the following day.

Can I cancel my order?

You can cancel or reschedule up to 24 hours before your slot.

How are points awarded?

Points are credited when your order reaches Completed status. Tiers: Carbon (0–99 pts), Stone (100–499 pts), Pearl (500+ pts).

What if I'm not happy with the clean?

Message us within 24 hours of getting your pair back. Before and after photos are taken for every order as a record.
