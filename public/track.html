Tracking FAQs

How long does the cleaning process take?

Standard services usually take a few days, while faster services may finish sooner. Your estimated ready date is shown in the order summary.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes, but only while the order is still in the early stages such as Booked or Received.

Will I receive notifications?

Email-style notifications are supported by the design. SMS can be added later as an extension.
